The problem

Revenue is easy to see. Profit isn't.

01

Fees hide in a dozen reports

Referral fees, fulfilment, storage, ad spend and refunds all land in different places, so nobody sees the full cost of a sale.

02

Month-end takes days

Someone rebuilds the P&L in a spreadsheet every month, and by the time it's done, the stock has already been reordered.

03

Money quietly leaks out

Underpaid reimbursements, pricier shipping than usual and products selling at a loss rarely show up until someone goes looking.

FAQ

Profitability questions.

Something else? Talk to our team.

What costs does the P&L include?+

Order-related and other marketplace fees, external and internal shipping, ad spend, refunds, product buying cost, operational costs, other expenses and OPEX, with profit and margin before and after tax/VAT.

What if I don't have product costs set up?+

WhatSales flags orders and products with missing cost of goods (COGS) and missing shipping charges, so you know exactly what to fill in before trusting a number.

Which marketplaces does Recovery cover?+

Amazon today. eBay, Etsy, Shopify, Temu and TikTok are coming soon.

Can reports be emailed automatically?+

Yes. Open any of the 27 reports and press Schedule to have it emailed on a timer, or export it to Excel any time.

Does it handle VAT?+

The P&L shows profit and margin before and after tax/VAT, and the Tax Liability report breaks down what you owe. Tax and VAT settings are configured per account.
