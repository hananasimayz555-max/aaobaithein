FAQ

Questions we get asked most.

Something else? Talk to our team.

Which marketplaces does WhatSales connect to?+

50+ integrations, including Amazon, eBay, Shopify, Walmart, Temu, TikTok Shop, B&Q, Tesco Marketplace, OnBuy, Debenhams and Etsy. WhatSales also works with Linnworks and Mirakl.

Do I have to use all nine products?+

No. Start with the product you need most and add more as you grow. They all share the same connected data.

Is there a free trial?+

Yes. Start a 7-day free trial, or book a demo to see WhatSales on your own data first.

Can I manage multiple brands or client accounts?+

Yes. See every brand and account in one view, then open any single one in a click. Agencies can give each client access to their own data only.

Which AI assistants work with WhatSales?+

Claude, ChatGPT and Grok connect through WhatSales MCP, so you can ask about sales, profit, ads and stock in plain English.
