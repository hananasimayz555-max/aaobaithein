FAQ

Questions sellers ask first.

Something else? Talk to the team.

How accurate is the profit figure?+

Every number is built from the line items your channels report: orders, fees, refunds and ad spend, plus your cost of goods and expenses. You can open any figure to see exactly what's inside it.

Which channels do you support?+

30+ direct and native integrations, including Amazon, eBay, Shopify, Walmart, Temu, TikTok Shop, B&Q, OnBuy, Etsy, WooCommerce and Linnworks.

How often does data sync?+

The Linnworks integration syncs roughly every 15–30 minutes, so your dashboard stays close to real time.

Do I need Linnworks?+

No. You can connect your channels directly. If you use Linnworks, WhatSales also pulls cost of goods, shipping and bundle data from it.

How much does it cost?+

Pricing is based on monthly order volume, billed monthly with no long-term lock-in. [Client to confirm starting price.]
