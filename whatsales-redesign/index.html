FAQ

Good questions.

Something else? Ask the team.

Which channels can I connect?+

30+, including Amazon, eBay, Shopify, Walmart, Temu, TikTok Shop, B&Q, Tesco Marketplace, OnBuy, Debenhams and Etsy. It also works with Linnworks and Mirakl.

How do I know the profit numbers are right?+

Every figure is built from the line items your channels report, plus your cost of goods and expenses. Open any number to see every order, fee and cost behind it.

Which AI assistants work with WhatSales?+

Claude, ChatGPT and Grok can connect to your WhatSales account, so you can ask about sales, profit, ads and stock in plain English.

Can I manage several brands or client accounts?+

Yes. See every brand and account in one view, then open any single one in a click. Agencies can give each client access to their own data only.
