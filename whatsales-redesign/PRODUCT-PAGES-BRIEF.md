# WhatSales product pages: content brief (6 products)

Source: `PLATFORM-RESEARCH.md` (read-only review of a live client account, Oct 2026). Everything here is something we saw in the app. **Check every line for accuracy before it goes live.** Lines marked ⚠ need confirmation from the product team.

## Shared page template

1. **Hero:** product icon and name, headline, subhead, Book a demo / Start 7-day free trial, product visual.
2. **The problem:** three pains in the seller's words.
3. **Inside the product:** one row per real screen or tab, alternating copy and visual, using the app's own labels.
4. **How it works:** 3 steps.
5. **Built on your data:** the integrations this product uses.
6. **Proof:** one relevant testimonial plus one stat.
7. **FAQ:** 5 questions, answered only with what the app does today.
8. **Related products** (the other 5) and the closing CTA.

Rules for all pages:
- Use the app's terms (P&L Statement, Product Diagnostics, Inventory Planning…), so what a prospect reads matches what they see after signup.
- All product visuals are illustrative and labelled as such until we have demo-account screenshots.
- Respect the app's stated limits (see each page's "Do not claim").

## 1. Profitability: "Profit, orders & costs"
**Headline:** Know what you really make, on every order.
**Subhead:** A monthly P&L built from sales, marketplace fees, ad spend, refunds, product cost and shipping, across every channel. Plus the money Amazon owes you and the costs you didn't know about.
**Inside:**
- **P&L Statement:** month-to-date plus five prior months side by side; per-unit view; monthly target; profit and margin before and after tax/VAT.
- **Orders:** profit and margin on every order; flags **Missing COGS** and **Missing Shipping Charges**.
- **Recovery:** "Money Amazon owes you — with the evidence to claim it back." Underpaid reimbursements, fee overcharges, lost & damaged, customer returns; plus "Fix your pricing": selling at a loss, margin squeezed, room to move.
- **Shipping Choice (NEW):** "When the same product goes out on a more expensive service than it usually does, you pay the difference for nothing. This page adds up what that cost."
- **Refunds:** total refunded value, most refunded reason, refunds trend over time.
- **Expenses:** costs no marketplace reports, by category, product or frequency.
- **Reports:** 27 ready-made reports, exported to Excel or scheduled by email.
**Integrations:** Amazon, Amazon Ads, eBay, Etsy, Shopify, Temu, TikTok Shop, Linnworks.
**Do not claim:** Recovery on marketplaces other than Amazon (shown as "SOON").

## 2. Performance: "Sales, ads & growth"
**Headline:** Find out what's working, what isn't, and what to fix first.
**Subhead:** Product, brand and campaign performance in one place, ranked by the money at stake, with the cause in plain English.
**Inside:**
- **Product Diagnostics:** "What is costing you money, on which products, and who should fix it." Problem tiles (losing money, ads not paying off, listing hijacked, too many returns, shoppers not buying, dead stock) with causes; hands fixes to Workspace.
- **Performance Tree (NEW):** browse by brand, category or gender, then drill into the products, plus the **Performance Copilot** chat ("Ask WhatSales").
- **Listing Health (NEW):** "Is every product listed on your channels, live, and up to each channel's rules?"
- **Buy Box Analysis (NEW):** "Why you win and lose the Buy Box, what it's costing you, and what to do next."
- **Ad Insights (NEW)** and **Budget Rules (BETA):** where ad money goes; no-code rules to raise budgets, pause at set hours or shape spend through the day.
- **SKU Performance, Comparison Charts, BSR Changes (BETA), Pricing Health (BETA), TikTok LIVE Insights (NEW).**
**Integrations:** Amazon, Amazon Ads, TikTok Shop, TikTok Ads, Linnworks.
**Do not claim:** Budget Rules on anything but Amazon Ads (eBay, TikTok, Walmart, Google "coming soon").

## 3. Inventory: "Stock, planning & POs"
**Headline:** Know what to order, how much, and when.
**Subhead:** Stock across your warehouses and Amazon, forecasts with a confidence level, and a buying list that tells you to order now, order by a date, or wait.
**Inside:**
- **Stock Overview:** "What you hold, where it is, what it is worth and how fast it sells."
- **Inventory Planning:** "What to order from suppliers and send to Amazon, and when." Order now / runs out before stock arrives / overstock; Supplier Purchasing (FBM) and Amazon Restock (FBA).
- **Purchase Orders:** plan supplier orders, track delivery dates, late and "arrives after stock-out".
- **Suppliers & Supplier Performance (BETA):** "Which suppliers earn their place, and which are costing you."
- **Price Checks (BETA):** catches supplier price jumps, one-offs and "cheapest ever".
**Integrations:** Linnworks (stock, POs, suppliers, warehouses), Amazon FBA.
**Do not claim:** WhatSales replacing Linnworks for stock control (stock owned by WhatSales is "COMING SOON").

## 4. Engage: "Support & reviews"
**Headline:** Every customer message in one inbox, with AI that knows when to hand over.
**Subhead:** Messages from every connected store, AI-drafted or AI-sent replies, no-code rules, refund approvals and Amazon review requests.
**Inside:**
- **Inbox:** "Every customer message, with what needs you first." AI drafts ready, handed to you, overdue.
- **AI Support (NEW):** three modes (off, AI drafts your team sends, live responses); choose request types; "Your team stays in control."
- **Rules (NEW)** and **Auto Responders:** reply, assign, set status, out-of-hours replies.
- **Process Refunds:** refund requests waiting for finance approval.
- **Feedback Automation (NEW):** uses Amazon's own "Request a Review"; "Nothing is ever sent twice."
- **Product Reviews & Negative Seller Feedback:** sentiment, recurring words.
**Integrations:** Amazon, eBay, Shopify, direct email, B&Q, Debenhams, Tesco, The Range.
**Do not claim:** customer satisfaction surveys (not available yet). ⚠ "Up to 80% of your inbox" is from the current website; confirm.

## 5. Flow: "Listings & publishing"
**Headline:** Publish listings everywhere, and prove they worked.
**Subhead:** Prepare, check and publish listings from one place. Then see whether they went live, and whether they helped search and sales.
**Inside:**
- **Flow Dashboard:** "Did your Flows go through on the marketplaces, and did they help?"
- **Flow Publisher:** "Prepare, validate, translate, and publish marketplace listings from one Flow."
- **Bulk Publisher (BETA):** "Change hundreds of listings at once. We check every one before anything is sent."
- **Product Studio, Channel Profiles, Amazon Brand Stories.**
**Integrations:** Amazon, eBay, Temu, Shopify, OnBuy, Mirakl retailers (Tesco, Debenhams, B&Q and more), Linnworks.

## 6. Workspace: "Tasks & team"
**Headline:** Turn every finding into owned, trackable work.
**Subhead:** A shared board for your team and ours, where problems found in WhatSales become tasks with an owner, a status and a hand-off.
**Inside:**
- **Board:** tasks moving through stages, review requests and "Ball With" hand-offs.
- **My Tasks / Support Tasks:** work by person, priority and status.
- **Listing Content:** listing image and content tasks, syncing from a Google Sheet.
- **Action Plan:** turn Product Diagnostics findings into tasks.
**Do not claim:** ⚠ "done-for-you team" details (from the current website; confirm what the service includes).
